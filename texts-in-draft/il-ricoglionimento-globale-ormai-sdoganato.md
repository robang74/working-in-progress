At that point the complexity we have created starts to fight back and civilisation collapses. In short: nature prevents too many stupid humans (in Cipolla terms, those who destroy value for nothing or worse, not low IQ) from growing up in adulthoods.

- https://lnkd.in/p/exTiQgi3

Scoutism is (or it was at that time) nothing else than a society abstraction to bring back kids in the wild and teach them the fundamentals. In particular, how to deal with their limits: how to learn he indispensable before unknown or supposition became an existential threat.

The most rooted objections against Land findings: 1. no peer reviewed data (NASA undisclosed); 2. the toddlers test isn't the same as the NASA test; 3. divergent thinking isn't creativity which also requires converging thinking and functional ability to exploit it. It seems a tragedy looking at the Land's findings (5yo 98%, 10yo: 30%, 15yo: 12%, and 25+: 2%) but the tragedy is limited to 12% to 2% (six times cut) because a 15yo human is supposed to be full functional in the wild (as biological being and social animal, not in modern terms of individualistic independence), at 25 just more experienced. That's the cut that matters the most.

The moneyless economy isn't free food for everyone, but the opposite is the sweet propaganda for which poverty is a cognitive status and such a poverty relies on money as fiat-currency (aka public debt).

Moreover, it is an audacious and dangerous bet that the transition from the economy, the illusion of economy due to financial replacement of the economy, toward the moneyless society (aka social score) could be driven without destroying civilization

The moneyless society is presented in an edulcolarated form of complacency as childhood intrusive thought like financial freedom by nothing (you will own nothing and you will be happy) but the counter-meme is the caveman explaining to the few survivors that for a little time a lot of "value" has been created for someone else.

Christmas brings gifts because someone built gifts and someone else bought gifts. Those gifts never appeared in the void, and kids stop to believe Santa the day they realise this as a fact: gifts aren't created by magic under the trees. Children did not arrive in the world from nothing.
